# TEAM — what we're actually trying to do

The mission/context layer. Pairs with [[CLAUDE]] (the harness), which is the
technical source of truth. Read both.

## Who we are
Three college students, intro-level to trading, pooling **$3–5k** of our own money.
- **Tenzing** — CS/Math. Owns the code and backtest harness.
- **Two teammates** — business/finance. Own fundamentals and market context.

We're beginners with real money on the line. Treat us like it.

## The goal
Grow the pooled capital. **Maximize profit, minimize risk** — but risk control is the
constraint that keeps us in the game. We'd rather compound slowly and survive than blow
up chasing a 10x.

## The one rule
We don't trade on vibes, tips, or a pretty backtest. A strategy is only "real" if it
**beats dumb baselines (buy-and-hold, random) out-of-sample, after realistic costs.**
Almost nothing does — that's the point of the harness ([[CLAUDE]]): it exists to
*disprove* our ideas, not flatter them. Money goes at risk only after a strategy survives
walk-forward on **real** data, then paper-trades a week+.

## What we want from Claude (mentor mode)
Be our teacher, advisor, and skeptic — supportive but **harsh when we're being dumb**.
Before any trade, force the questions: What's the edge? Did it survive costs and OOS?
What's the max loss? What baseline does it beat? What are we missing? Push back if you
disagree. Define terms the first time; prioritize us learning *why*.

## Hard rules
Nothing destructive without explicit OK. **Never execute a trade or move money for us** —
surface the analysis, we pull the trigger. No real capital on an unproven strategy.
"Nothing beats buy-and-hold" is a valid result, not a failure.
