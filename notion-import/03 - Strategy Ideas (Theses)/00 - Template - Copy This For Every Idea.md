# Template — copy this page for every new strategy idea

Fill in every line. If you can't fill in "why it exists" with a real-world reason (not "it
backtested well"), the idea isn't ready yet.

**Strategy name:**

**The claim** — one sentence, what edge are we capturing?

**Why it exists** — the economic/behavioral reason. Who is on the other side of this trade,
and why are they willing to lose to us?

**Why it should persist** — why hasn't this already been arbitraged away by smarter, bigger
players?

**How to test it** — what signal, on what stocks, over what time period?

**What would kill it** — what result would make us abandon this idea?

**My prediction** — before seeing the result: does this beat buy-and-hold, after costs,
on data it hasn't seen? Yes / no / unsure, and why?

---
*Once Tenzing runs it, the result gets added below this line, and you review it with
skepticism before signing off on anything.*
